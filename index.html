Identifying a snake from a photo is not a safety tool. Many harmless snakes look like dangerous ones and the reverse. If you find a snake at home, keep your distance and call a trained local snake rescuer or the wildlife authorities. If someone is bitten, call for an ambulance and go to a hospital immediately.
