How to identify

A Zayu Mountain Pit Viper

Venomous: a bite needs urgent medical care.

  • A stout, brown or pinkish-brown pit viper with dark brown blotches along the back and sides.
  • A short triangular head with a heat-sensing pit, vertical pupils and a plain pale belly.
  • Very like the Mountain Pit Viper. Venomous.
