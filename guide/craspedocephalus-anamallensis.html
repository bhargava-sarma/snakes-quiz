How to identify

An Anamalai Pit Viper

Venomous: a bite needs urgent medical care.

  • Stocky, mottled pit viper in olive-brown, yellow-brown, orange-brown or greenish tones, with dark speckles and blotches.
  • Broad triangular head with a rounded snout, vertical pupils and a pit between eye and nostril.
  • Found only in the high hills of the southern Western Ghats, usually coiled on a branch.

Did you know? This pit viper lives only in a small area of high hills in the southern Western Ghats.
