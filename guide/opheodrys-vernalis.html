Smooth Greensnake

Opheodrys vernalis

Non-venomousNorth AmericaColubridaeDifficulty

How to identify

A Smooth Greensnake

Non-venomous.

  • Small and bright plain green, with a white or pale yellow belly.
  • Smooth, unridged scales.
  • Lives on the ground in grass rather than in bushes.

Did you know? After it dies its green colour fades to blue, because the yellow pigment breaks down first.

Look-alikesand how to tell them apart

Rough GreensnakeOpheodrys aestivus

The Rough Greensnake is larger and slimmer, lives in shrubs and has keeled scales; the Smooth Greensnake is small, lives in grass and has smooth scales.

Harmless

Green RatsnakeSenticolis triaspis

The Green Ratsnake has a head clearly broader than its neck, whereas the Smooth Greensnake has a narrow head barely wider than its neck.

Harmless

Common Sharp-tailed SnakeContia tenuis

The Common Sharp-tailed Snake is mostly brown, red and grey, whereas the Smooth Greensnake is mostly green.

Harmless

Forest Sharp-tailed SnakeContia longicauda

The Forest Sharp-tailed Snake is mostly brown and red, whereas the Smooth Greensnake is mostly green.

Quick facts

Length
0.3-0.5 m (12-20 in)
Habitat
Meadows, prairie, open grassy woodland and marsh edges
Found in
Southern Canada (Nova Scotia to Saskatchewan) and the northern USA, south along the Appalachians; isolated groups in the west
Eats
Caterpillars, crickets, grasshoppers and spiders
Active
By day
Family
Colubridae (colubrids (rat snakes, racers, kingsnakes, garter & water snakes ...))
