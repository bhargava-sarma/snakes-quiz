How to identify

A Diard's Blind Snake

Non-venomous.

  • A large, thick-bodied blind snake: shiny brown to dark grey above, often paler below, and much thicker than the tiny Brahminy Blind Snake.
  • A blunt, rounded head with no neck and tiny dot-like eyes under the scales; a very short tail ending in a small spine.
  • Harmless; may turn up above ground after heavy rain.

Quick facts

Length
25-40 cm, rarely up to 50 cm
Habitat
Soil and leaf litter, and under logs and stones, in forest, farmland and gardens
Found in
North-east India (Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura and northern West Bengal), with scattered records elsewhere, and South-East Asia
Eats
Ants and termites, and their eggs and larvae
Active
Underground; comes up at night and after rain
Family
Typhlopidae (blind snakes)
