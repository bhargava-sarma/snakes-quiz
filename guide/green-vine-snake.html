Green Vine Snake

Ahaetulla nasuta complex

Also called Common Green Whip Snake, Long-nosed Whip Snake

Mildly venomousIndiaColubridaeDifficulty

© Nathan Earley (@earley_bird)·CC BY·iNaturalist·Kerala·iNat ID: Ahaetulla malabarica

How to identify

A Green Vine Snake

Mildly venomous (rear-fanged): not considered dangerous to people.

  • Extremely slender, long, bright leaf-green body (sometimes yellowish or grey-brown) that looks just like a vine.
  • Long, pointed head with a snout that ends in a sharp tip.
  • Horizontal, keyhole-shaped pupils, which set vine snakes apart from almost every other snake in India.
  • A pale yellow or white line along the lower flank on each side; black and white skin between the scales shows when it puffs up its neck.
  • Sways slowly like a twig in the wind and may gape to show a dark mouth lining.

Did you know? The horizontal pupils give vine snakes forward-facing vision so they can judge the distance to a lizard before they strike. Their mild venom is harmless to people.

Look-alikesand how to tell them apart

Bamboo Pit ViperCraspedocephalus gramineus

The Bamboo Pit Viper is stout enough to look rope-like, with a broad triangular head, vertical pupils and a heat pit. The Green Vine Snake is extremely thin with a long pointed head, keyhole-shaped horizontal pupils and no pit.

Venomous

Yellow-green Cat SnakeBoiga flaviviridis

The Green Vine Snake is extremely slender with a long, pointed head and keyhole-shaped horizontal pupils. The Yellow-green Cat Snake has a heavier body, a broad blunt head distinct from the neck, and vertical pupils.

Mild venom

Common Bronzeback Tree SnakeDendrelaphis tristis complex

Both are slender tree snakes. The Green Vine Snake is leaf-green with a pointed snout and horizontal, keyhole-shaped pupils. The Bronzeback is bronze-brown with big round eyes, a black eye stripe and a rounded head.

Mild venom

Green KeelbackRhabdophis plumbicolor

The Green Keelback is a stout, short, ground-living snake with a broad head and dark neck markings. The Green Vine Snake is a very slim tree snake with a long, pointed head.

Mild venom

Quick facts

Length
1-1.5 m, up to about 2 m
Habitat
Shrubs, hedges, gardens, scrub and forest; lives in bushes and low trees
Found in
Most of peninsular India, the Western and Eastern Ghats and the north-east (several very similar species share this green vine-snake look, so they are grouped here)
Eats
Garden lizards and other lizards, frogs and sometimes small birds
Active
By day
Family
Colubridae (colubrids (rat snakes, racers, kingsnakes, garter & water snakes ...))
