Sonoran Coral Snake

Micruroides euryxanthus

Also called Arizona Coral Snake, Western Coral Snake

VenomousNorth AmericaElapidaeDifficulty

How to identify

A Sonoran Coral Snake

Venomous: a bite needs urgent medical care.

  • A small coral snake with wide red and black rings separated by white or pale yellow rings.
  • A black head with a pale band behind it.
  • Red touches the pale rings.

Did you know? When alarmed it makes a popping sound by pushing air out of its vent.

Look-alikesand how to tell them apart

Sonoran Mountain KingsnakeLampropeltis pyromelana

The Sonoran Coral Snake is small, with a black head, wide red and black rings and pale rings that touch the red. The Sonoran Mountain Kingsnake has a white snout and red touching black.

Harmless

Western GroundsnakeSonora semiannulata

Banded Western Groundsnakes have black bands over an orange body but no pale rings, and the bands do not cross the belly. The Sonoran Coral Snake has full rings of red, black and pale yellow, and a black head.

Harmless

Harlequin Coral SnakeMicrurus fulvius
