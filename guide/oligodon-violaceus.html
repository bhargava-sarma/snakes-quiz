How to identify

A Coral Red Kukri Snake

Non-venomous.

  • A strikingly plain, bright coral-red to orange kukri snake, sometimes with a darker reddish or purplish tinge on the back.
  • A short head with a blunt snout and small dark eyes; smooth, glossy scales.
  • Until recently called the Kheri Kukri Snake (Oligodon kheriensis). Harmless.
