Glossy Crayfish Snake

Liodytes rigida

Non-venomousNorth AmericaColubridaeDifficulty

© @snorbertz·CC BY-NC·iNaturalist·Georgia

How to identify

A Glossy Crayfish Snake

Non-venomous.

  • Glossy brown with faint darker stripes.
  • A yellow belly with two rows of dark spots.
  • Smooth, shiny scales.

Did you know? It is so secretive that it is mostly found by searching wetland plants at night.

Look-alikesand how to tell them apart

Mississippi Green WatersnakeNerodia cyclopion

The Mississippi Green Watersnake has rough, keeled scales, whereas the Glossy Crayfish Snake has smooth, glossy scales.

Harmless

Pine Woods LittersnakeRhadinaea flavilata

The Pine Woods Littersnake has a narrow head barely wider than its neck, whereas the Glossy Crayfish Snake has a head clearly broader than its neck.

Mild venom

Plain-bellied WatersnakeNerodia erythrogaster

The Plain-bellied Watersnake has rough, keeled scales, whereas the Glossy Crayfish Snake has smooth, glossy scales.

Harmless
