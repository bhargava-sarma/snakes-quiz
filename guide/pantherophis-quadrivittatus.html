Yellow Ratsnake

Pantherophis quadrivittatus

Also called Eastern Ratsnake (coastal plain), Everglades Ratsnake, Chicken Snake

Non-venomousNorth AmericaColubridaeDifficulty

How to identify

A Yellow Ratsnake

Non-venomous.

  • Yellow, orange or olive with four dark stripes running along the body.
  • In the north of its range many are darker or blackish with faint stripes.
  • A long climber with a squarish body and a pale, unmarked chin.
  • Young are grey with dark blotches.

Did you know? The orange "Everglades" form of south Florida is the most colourful ratsnake in North America.

Quick facts

Length
1.1-1.8 m (3.5-6 ft)
Habitat
Pine woods, hammocks, swamps, farmland and suburbs of the coastal plain
Found in
The south-eastern coastal plain, from Virginia and North Carolina to Florida and the Gulf coast
Eats
Rodents, birds and their eggs, and frogs
Active
By day and at night
Family
Colubridae (colubrids (rat snakes, racers, kingsnakes, garter & water snakes ...))
