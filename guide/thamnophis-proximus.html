How to identify

A Western Ribbonsnake

Non-venomous.

  • Very slender, with a long tail about a third of its length.
  • A bright orange or yellow stripe down the back and yellow side stripes on a black or brown body.
  • Plain, unmarked pale lips, and a white mark in front of the eye.
  • Keeled scales and large eyes.

Did you know? Ribbonsnakes often flee across the surface of the water and into vegetation rather than diving.

Look-alikesand how to tell them apart

Common Garter SnakeThamnophis sirtalis

The Western Ribbonsnake is slimmer, with a long tail, plain pale lips and usually an orange back stripe; the Common Garter Snake is stockier with barred lips.

Harmless

Aquatic Garter SnakeThamnophis atratus

The Aquatic Garter Snake lives in or beside water, whereas the Western Ribbonsnake is equally at home on the ground and in bushes and trees.

Eats
