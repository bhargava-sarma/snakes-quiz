How to identify

A Nicobar Cat Snake

Mildly venomous (rear-fanged): not considered dangerous to people.

  • A medium-sized cat snake known only from the Nicobar Islands: yellowish to olive, often darker on the back, with a yellow chin and belly.
  • A broad head clearly wider than the neck, a fairly long snout and big eyes with vertical pupils.
  • Mildly venomous (rear-fanged) but harmless to people.

Look-alikesand how to tell them apart

Many-banded Cat SnakeBoiga multifasciata

The Many-banded Cat Snake has distinct cross-bands along the body, whereas the Nicobar Cat Snake has a plain, unmarked body.

Mild venom

Green Cat SnakeBoiga cyanea

The Green Cat Snake lives up in bushes and trees, whereas the Nicobar Cat Snake is equally at home on the ground and in bushes and trees.

Mild venom

Andaman Cat SnakeBoiga andamanensis

The Andaman Cat Snake is mostly tan, brown and red, whereas the Nicobar Cat Snake is mostly yellow and olive.

Mild venom

Assamese Cat SnakeBoiga quincunciata

The Assamese Cat Snake has a spotted pattern, whereas the Nicobar Cat Snake has a plain, unmarked body.

Mild venom
