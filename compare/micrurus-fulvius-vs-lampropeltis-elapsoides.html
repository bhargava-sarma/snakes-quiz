Harlequin Coral Snake vs Scarlet Kingsnake

How to tell them apart, side by side.

Harlequin Coral Snake

Micrurus fulvius
Highly venomousN. America

Scarlet Kingsnake

Lampropeltis elapsoides
Non-venomousN. America

What sets them apart

On the coral snake, red touches yellow and the snout is black. On the Scarlet Kingsnake, red touches black and the snout is red. (The rhyme only works for snakes native to the USA.)

The Scarlet Kingsnake is not dangerous, but the Harlequin Coral Snake is highly venomous.

Recognising the Harlequin Coral Snake

  • Glossy rings of red, yellow and black around the body; the red rings touch the narrow yellow rings.
  • A blunt black snout, then a wide yellow band across the back of the head.
  • Slim, smooth and shiny, with a small head no wider than the neck.
  • The tail has only black and yellow rings.

Recognising the Scarlet Kingsnake

  • Small, with rings of red, black and yellow or white; red touches black.
  • A red snout (the coral snake's snout is black).
  • Glossy and slim.
